06 / Voice

Curious, assured,
plainspoken.

Say what it is. Then stop.

Sounds like Standard Issue

“Claims: None. It's a T-shirt.”

Short, honest, a little dry. Facts over adjectives.

Doesn't sound like Standard Issue

Hype, urgency or borrowed prestige: “limited drop”, “luxury cotton”, “everyone's wearing it”. We can't back those up, so we don't say them.

A living reference. Change a shared token or the logo file, and the storefront and this board change together. Compare them at the same width to check they still match.
