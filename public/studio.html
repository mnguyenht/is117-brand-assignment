The challenge

Design changes
what a product means.

The product is intentionally ordinary. That makes your choices easier to see.

Learn to brief AI, compare alternatives, justify design choices, and check the code against your decisions.

A persona gives a choice a purpose. An archetype gives the brand a personality. Layout, type, color, logo, and language turn those ideas into something someone can experience.

You will work in small cycles with an AI coach. You make the decisions; the coach helps you explore, build, and check them. The README is the single setup and assignment path.

The studio at a glance

From a person
to a page.

Learn each idea when it helps your next choice. Return to earlier decisions when trying the website teaches you something new.

  1. 01 / Try

    Try the baseline.

    Explore the working storefront and its demo bag. Agree on the first feature: your branded storefront and live style guide.

    Try: a functional starting page

  2. 02 / Decide

    Decide who and why.

    Make two small persona cards: a primary need to guide the page and a contrasting need to check it. Build an early brief with one primary archetype and voice traits.

    Make: personas + an early brief

  3. 03 / Compare

    Compare two small previews.

    Compare modernist and postmodernist heroes using the same product, copy, and action. Use real design references, then choose or adjust a direction.

    Choose: a direction with reasons

  4. 04 / Build

    Build the chosen slice.

    Create a simple original SVG logo and apply the identity to the page and live style guide. Make two truthful, useful applications of Cialdini’s persuasion principles.

    Make: logo + guide + branded page

  5. 05 / Check

    Check together.

    Let AI run checks and review the change. Explain one actual test, then inspect the browser and describe what you observe.

    Check: code + your browser observation

  6. 06 / Keep

    Keep a change you understand.

    Save decisions and evidence in your SPEC, review the feature’s pull request, then decide whether to keep and merge the checked change in your repository.

    Keep: decisions + evidence + working code

Two ways to
organize a message.

These small studies introduce a contrast, not a finished brand. Try both approaches with your own audience in mind.

Modernist approach

Explore a clear grid, restrained elements, and a direct hierarchy.

Postmodernist approach

Explore contrast, layering, mixed references, and a more expressive hierarchy.

Simplified visual studies. Neither approach automatically fits every audience; explain what your comparison reveals.

A conversation, paced for you

You decide.
Your coach helps.

Your AI coach should ask one small question or offer one small step, then wait for your actual response. Ask for a hint or example when you need one.

Before a build, make a prediction. After AI checks, discuss one test that actually ran. In the browser, describe what you observe. Save what you agree in SPEC.md.

You can adopt an AI idea; explain why it helps your visitor. The coach keeps a short, AI-drafted LEARNING.md summary of your contributions and help received, not a full transcript. It may be public with your repository; review, correct or remove excerpts before they are committed or pushed. Your instructor reviews it with the project using a simple three-part rubric; the AI does not assign your grade.

After README setup, keep npm run dev running: saved page and asset edits refresh the local browser automatically and reset the demo bag. Open the server's printed address. npm start does the same. Stop the server with Ctrl+C before switching to optional npm run preview, which needs manual refresh. Reopen the printed address after switching. Local edits do not publish to GitHub Pages.
