In this policy, "we" means the person named above and "you" means anyone using the site.

## What we collect

**You give it to us:**

- Your **email address and a password** when you create an account. We never see your password; our sign-in provider stores only a scrambled (hashed) version.
- Your **shipping name and address** (street, city, postal code, country) when you check out. We save them with your demo order.

**Created as you use the site:**

- Your **saved bag** (sizes and quantities) and your **demo order history**.

**Collected automatically:**

- Our hosting and sign-in providers record **IP addresses and basic request details** in their logs, to run and protect the service.

We do not collect payment details. Checkout has no card fields.

## How we use it

- To sign you in and keep your bag between visits.
- To record your demo order and show it in your account.
- To send you one confirmation email per order.
- To keep the site running and stop abuse.

We do not send marketing email.

## Who we share it with

We use these services, and each gets only what it needs:

- **Supabase** (accounts and database): your email, hashed password, saved bag, orders and shipping address.
- **Resend** (email delivery): your email address and the order summary (order number, sizes, quantities, total). While our email account is in test mode, the copy is sent to the store owner instead of you.
- **Vercel** (hosting): request logs, including IP addresses.

**We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.**

## Cookies and tracking

We use no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your sign-in session and, before you sign in, your bag. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Because we do no cross-site tracking, there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to turn off. We treat both as already honored.

## How long we keep it

- **Account, saved bag and orders:** until you delete your account (see below), or until the course project is shut down, whichever comes first.
- **Provider logs:** for as long as Supabase and Vercel keep them, which is days to weeks under their standard settings.

## Your choices

- **See your orders:** sign in and open **Account**.
- **Change your password:** **Account → Change password**.
- **Delete your account:** email [ASK: contact email] from your account's address. We delete the account, and that removes your saved bag and every order linked to it.
- For anything else, email the same address.

## Security

- The site is served only over HTTPS.
- Passwords are handled by Supabase and stored hashed.
- Database rules mean each account can read only its own bag and orders.
- Prices are checked on the server.
- Secret keys stay on the server, never in the site's code.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

## Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If the change is material, we will also email account holders before it takes effect.
