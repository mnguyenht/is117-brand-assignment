# A simple assessment

Look at the student's project, `SPEC.md`, issue/PR, and short [LEARNING.md](../LEARNING.md) record together. Assess the choices and evidence, not the number of prompts, writing length, polished AI prose, or agreement with the agent. Hints and examples are welcome; disclose the help rather than treating it as a failure.

| Criterion                        | Clear evidence                                                                                                                               | Some evidence                                                                                           | Not yet evidenced                                                                |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Make and explain decisions       | The student connects an audience need to a visible choice and explains why it may help.                                                      | A choice and audience are identified, but their connection needs clarification.                         | Only approval, AI recommendations, or an unexplained choice is available.        |
| Build a coherent, truthful brand | The previews, logo, storefront, and live guide express the agreed identity; references and persuasion claims are supportable.                | The intended identity is visible, with specific inconsistencies or unsupported claims still to address. | The required work or enough evidence to inspect it is unavailable.               |
| Check and improve the result     | The student explains what one actual check establishes, reports a browser observation, and gives an evidence-based reason to revise or keep. | Checks or observations exist, but their meaning or the resulting decision needs clarification.          | Only a passing-check claim, AI observation, or unreasoned approval is available. |

These are human judgment descriptions, not automatic grades. The AI may point to evidence and identify gaps; it must not assign a score or certify understanding. A missing statement is not proof that the student cannot explain it.

## A quick instructor review

1. Open the site and guide, then skim the linked SPEC and PR for the promised choices and actual checks.
2. Read the four contribution rows in LEARNING.md. Distinguish the student's words from AI help and tool results. A bare “yes,” copied explanation, or approval of the summary does not establish understanding.
3. If evidence is missing, ask one concrete follow-up about the student's actual page or test. Allow an explanation or demonstration; use the response to clarify the assessment.

## What the record means

LEARNING.md is a small AI-drafted record of task-relevant contributions available to the agent, not a transcript or an automatic recording system. It does not automatically include other chats or the desktop's conversation history. Quotes must be real; paraphrases and assistance must be labeled. Unavailable evidence stays pending. Neither the record nor passing checks alone proves authorship or understanding.

Students can correct or remove excerpts before sharing. Keep personal details and secrets out: files committed to a public repository are public. Review the record for accuracy with the student; their approval confirms accuracy, not learning.

For optional instructor calibration, see the [full coached trial and worked rubric example](full-coached-trial-2026-10-25.md); its learner contributions are role-play, not required student work.
